Haystack Labs 2026

Deer Isle, ME

I had the privledge of co-facilitating an event (charrette?) called Haystack Labs in 2026, along side Janet Hollingsworth from The Metro. We were joined and supported by many kind people from Haystack Mountain School of Crafts. James Rutter and Phoebe Zildjian from the Fab Lab were terrific hosts and so much more; thank you both for everything that you do.

The event took place with 38 participants on Haystack's campus September 10-12, 2026, and included dedicated travel days on the 9th and the 13th. Documentation by participants (and co-facilitators) is still very much a work-in-progress, so for now, I'll share a few pictures of my work. Much more to come from everyone else!!
